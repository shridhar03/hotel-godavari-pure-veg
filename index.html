Our Story

Where the river teaches us to serve

For over two decades, our kitchen has followed the rhythm of the Godavari: slow, generous and pure. Every meal is 100% vegetarian, cooked fresh each morning with seasonal produce from farms along the river.

Satvik by Tradition

Food that nourishes body and mind

Our satvik recipes come from grandmothers' kitchens in Nashik and Paithan. Jain and no onion-garlic versions are always available, and they are never an afterthought.

Atithi Devo Bhava

The guest is god

From a welcome glass of sol kadhi to a quiet room facing the evening aarti, we look after you the way a family looks after its own.
